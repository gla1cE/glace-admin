Reading the metrics: Group avg spread measures balance across groups (lower = groups are evenly matched). Range and SD inside a group measure balance within it. Per-matchup gaps show the Elo difference each round-robin game would have \u2014 a high max gap means at least one lopsided match even if the group average looks fair. Snake draft equalizes group averages first; it does not minimize internal gaps.
